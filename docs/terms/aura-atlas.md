# Term: AURA Atlas

## Plain Meaning

AURA Atlas is the persistent evidence map.

It stores, scopes, correlates, and explains historical evidence over time.

## Relationship To AURA-Sense

```txt
AURA-Sense observes now.
AURA Atlas remembers later.
```

AURA-Sense may hand off or align with Atlas, but it should not become Atlas inside the HUD.
