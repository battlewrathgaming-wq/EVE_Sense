# Term: AURA-7

## Plain Meaning

AURA-7 is the tactical viewport.

It helps the player understand what is happening now and what may require immediate attention.

## What It Is

- tactical overlay
- transient telemetry witness
- low-cognitive-load HUD
- scoped tactical inspection tool

## What It Is Not

AURA-7 is not a historical intelligence platform, permanent evidence warehouse, or broad scraping system.

Long-term memory belongs to AURA Atlas.
